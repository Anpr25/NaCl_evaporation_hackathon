# Field Change Notice FCN-31
Status: APPROVED. Date: 2026-08-14. Raised by: Mechanical. Approved by: Project Engineer.
Supersedes: the envelope conductance stated in 4471-M-01 Rev A (design basis) and in the
equipment schedule.

## Reason

Post-installation thermographic survey of the North Wing found the envelope performing worse
than the Rev A design allowance. Measured lumped conductance for zone ZN-1 is 500 W/K, not
the 450 W/K carried in Rev A and in the equipment schedule.

## Change

| Item | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| EF-1 envelope conductance | 450 W/K | 500 W/K |

No other values change. The preheat coil duty is unaffected and remains as scheduled; the
coil was sized with margin and reheat capacity is still adequate at the revised loss.

## Action

All heat loss calculations and any dynamic models of ZN-1 shall use 500 W/K. The design basis
and the equipment schedule will be reissued at the next revision; until then this notice is
the governing value.
